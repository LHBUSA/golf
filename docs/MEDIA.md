# Media rights

Pipeline: discover → capture file-level rights → verify author/licence → verify entity identity → review non-copyright restrictions → bounded download/hash → immutable original → versioned local AVIF/WebP derivatives → approved manifest → explicit dimensions/credit.

Store source, URL, author, licence/licence URL, attribution, entity/editorial-only scope, identity proof, cached assets, hash, dimensions, transformations, review version/date and rights status. Revocation removes use and invalidates cache, retaining audit evidence under reviewed retention policy.

[Commons guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) requires individual licence and non-copyright review. CC0/verified public domain first. CC BY/SA owner policy review precedes accepting attribution/share-alike obligations. Government imagery is not blanket public domain: contractors, jurisdiction, people and logos matter. Media portals/organization photos do not imply redistribution rights.

No hotlinked photos. No fake athlete imagery or generated courses. Missing player photo uses original PBE initials/identity treatment; no guessed faces. Course/tournament assignment requires actual location proof.

No new commercial contractual obligation from CC0 asset reuse.

Production assets should be content-addressed in owner-approved R2/CDN with immutable derivative caching. Player-photo/venue-photo breadth remains future rights work. Editorial and promotion rights are evaluated separately.
