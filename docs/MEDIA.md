# Media rights

Pipeline: discover → capture file-level rights → verify author/licence → verify entity identity → review non-copyright restrictions → bounded download/hash → immutable original → versioned local AVIF/WebP derivatives → approved manifest → explicit dimensions/credit.

Store source, URL, author, licence/licence URL, attribution, entity/editorial-only scope, identity proof, cached assets, hash, dimensions, transformations, review version/date and rights status. Revocation removes use and invalidates cache, retaining audit evidence under reviewed retention policy.

[Commons guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) requires individual licence and non-copyright review. CC0/verified public domain first. CC BY/SA owner policy review precedes accepting attribution/share-alike obligations. Government imagery is not blanket public domain: contractors, jurisdiction, people and logos matter. Media portals/organization photos do not imply redistribution rights.

No hotlinked photos. No fake athlete imagery or generated courses. Missing player photo uses original PBE initials/identity treatment; no guessed faces. Course/tournament assignment requires actual location proof.

Candidate homepage photograph: [Golf Course Sunrise](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Golf_course_sunrise_-_Panorama_(Dimitrios_Savva_and_Jarod_Guest_via_Poly_Haven).jpg), Dimitrios Savva (photography), Jarod Guest (processing), [Poly Haven CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Described as a real photographic panorama. Use only after local manifest/download proof. Editorial-only: named course unknown, not a live venue. Any projection/crop is recorded. No new commercial contractual obligation from CC0 asset reuse.

Production assets should be content-addressed in owner-approved R2/CDN with immutable derivative caching. Player-photo/venue-photo breadth remains future rights work. Editorial and promotion rights are evaluated separately.


## Tournament hero course photography

Tournament heroes use the actual championship venue whenever an exact, rights-cleared course photograph exists. Rendering order: exact approved course photograph, then the normal branded dark hero. Generic golf imagery never stands in for a named venue.

Exact venue imagery can enter the Commons lane through the entity's verified Wikidata P18 image or a reviewed curated-course record whose Commons description and location independently prove the photographed course. Curated records still pass the same file-level licence allowlist, immutable capture/hash, derivative generation, per-image credit and revocation workflow. A press gallery or organization website alone is not sufficient redistribution permission.

The first curated exact-course record is Black Desert Resort Golf Course: Wikimedia Commons file `Firefly autonomous lawn mowers at a golf course.jpg`, photographed at the Black Desert Championship fairway in Ivins, Utah, CC0 1.0, with coordinates inside the reviewed course geometry.
