# Vision

PropBetEdge Golf Intelligence connects careers, courses, tournament editions and championship history. Its value is context a scores page cannot supply. The product should be cinematic, dense and fast while retaining independent PBE identity.

PGA TOUR is the men's center of gravity. LPGA and the five women's majors share every scoring, history, rankings, newsroom, media and analytics contract. Global coverage expands only where legitimate sources support it. Missing information is a coverage fact.

Public discovery offers useful scores, news and entity context when available. All Access / Pro Club grants advanced PBEcast, Player DNA, Course DNA, Course Fit and historical intelligence. No golf subscription exists.

Pine, charcoal, warm ivory and restrained gold form the design system. Scorecard rules, original contours and editorial typography establish identity. Photography must be real and individually rights-approved. Do not imply tour affiliation or label editorial imagery as a live venue.

Today connects events to context; Live prioritizes observed scoring; Majors treats both divisions intentionally; PBEcast combines active-hole context, leaderboard and pulse. Players and courses become historical experiences after sufficient data exists. Gear remains architecture-only with promotion disclosure.

Success requires credible provenance, meaningful history, understandable reasoning, server authorization, mobile accessibility and immediate shells. Fabricated density and meaningless generated stories defeat the product.
